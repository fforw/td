# Roadmap

What isn't built yet, by topic. The details are under `## Planned` in
each file.

## Open questions

- The [economy](economy.md) beyond gold: resources, facilities, and what
  buildings cost.
- What wisdom reveals in an explorer's report, exactly.
- How heroes die, beyond falling and being carried out.
- Whether gear found in levels gets robbed like gold when a hero is
  carried out, see [Gear](action/gear.md#planned).
- Whether rising campaign levels switch spawn groups to more skilled
  ones at set thresholds, or harder play just comes from monster skill
  and groups rising together.

## Planned

- [Monsters](action/monsters.md#planned): saved monsters, inhabitants
  from the story, a spread of skill, trees for more monsters.
- [Combat](action/combat.md#planned): ways of dying, dying heroes.
- [Dungeon generation](dungeon/generation.md#planned): premises, room
  splitting, loops, roles and content, locks and keys, rebaking lights.
- [View of a level](dungeon/view.md#planned): calmer wall cuts,
  monster silhouettes.
- [Spawns](dungeon/spawns.md#planned): groups by role, the end boss,
  escalation, room roles.
- [Fixtures](dungeon/fixtures.md#planned): fixtures beyond chests,
  traps.
- [Levels](world/levels.md#planned): level records, the face and edge
  overlay, themes, portals, doors.
- [Campaign](world/campaign.md#planned): opening situation, palette,
  bonuses with campaign levels, boss experience.
- [Discovery](world/discovery.md#planned): director weighting, wisdom,
  cost tables per traveller, event log.
- [Buildings](world/buildings.md#planned): facilities, house interiors.
- [Story](story/story.md): situations, stereotypes, arcs and the
  director, none built yet.
- [Glyphs](story/glyphs.md#planned): glyphs in the game, channels,
  exposure through vignettes.
- [Scenes](story/scenes.md#planned): more triggers, marks, beats and
  scenes.
- [Controls](action/controls.md#planned): weapons for all heroes.
- [Gear](action/gear.md#planned): elements that do something, a pose
  for throwing, light from balls, more pieces.
- [Threats](threats.md): grudges, knowledge, search, bandits, none built
  yet.
- [Economy](economy.md#planned): resources, facilities, consumers.
- [Sound](sound.md#planned): group volumes, walking distance, blows of monsters, moving cuboids.
