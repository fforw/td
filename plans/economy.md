# Economy

## Gold

- Gold coins are the one simple countable valuable so far. A campaign
  starts with 1000 gold (`START_GOLD`).
- Hiring explorers costs gold (`price()` in `game/tavern.ts`). There is
  no spending limit, and the balance may go negative.
- Heroes find gold in chests and bring it home, or spend it at the
  [trader](story/scenes.md#the-trader).
