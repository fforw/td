Treasure & Dread · glyph language

Stone script

Each glyph is built from the stroke order and stroke types of a source character. The layout of the source stays, the shapes change. Every stroke becomes one tile, picked by the kind of stroke and sized by its length. The polygon tiles follow the regular n-gons of gen-100. Every root is composed once and reused, so 心 looks the same in heart, anger, love and fear. Roots are laid out one level deep, so nested parts stay large. Like in gen-100, each polygon is set face to face against the one before it where there is room, on a side picked by the world seed. Each world also deals the polygons out anew, and the stroke kind left with no polygon becomes a line below its group: one game world, one fixed form per root. Colors come from one of the curated palettes: a tile is drawn in the main color 65% of the time, the alternate color 30% and the accent 5%. Roots and tiles settle around each other without overlapping; roots keep a little more distance, joined by a short dash across the gap, and a loose stroke next to a root is joined to it by a dot.
